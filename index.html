Security

Your keys never leave your wallet.

BLARC only knows your public address. Trades are requested through WalletConnect and signed by you. If something can't be prepared safely, nothing is sent.

Non-custodial

BLARC holds no private keys and cannot move funds on its own.

WalletConnect

Pair the wallet you already use. Each trade is a request you approve or reject.

Seed shown once

A wallet created in the bot shows its recovery phrase one time, then it is forgotten. Only the public address is kept.

Anti-phishing

Official links live in the bot's Support screen. BLARC will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, or login code.
